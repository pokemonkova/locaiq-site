HOW IT WORKS

We don’t estimate occupancy — we measure it.

Listings show what’s on offer. We show where people actually live.

1

Data collection

We obtain independent data on how properties are actually used, with the consent of owners and management companies.

2

Occupancy detection

Our algorithm analyses usage patterns to determine whether each unit is occupied or vacant, and whether it’s a long-term or short-term rental.

3

Market cross-check

We add the number of sale and rental listings, arrivals statistics and transaction data.

4

Aggregation

The map and subscription show data by complex. Unit-level detail is available only in reports.

5

Updates

Data is refreshed every two weeks.

Privacy

We operate under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). We never publish personal data of residents or owners.

Honest about accuracy

Our verified data layer is in pilot and building its history. Accuracy and depth grow with every season.

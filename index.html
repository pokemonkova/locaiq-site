METHOD

Measured, not self-reported.

Utility-based

Occupancy is derived from electricity consumption, not from listings or owner claims.

Stated precision

Typical margin of error is ±5 percentage points. We say so in every report.

Private by design

We publish levels and ranks, never meter data or personal details.

FAQ

Questions we hear most

How do you measure occupancy?

We compare the monthly electricity use of each house with its own empty and fully occupied levels. The result is an estimate of how many days a house was lived in.

Why don’t you show exact percentages on the map?

The free map shows five levels only. Exact percentages, days per month and house-by-house figures are part of the paid reports.

Which areas do you cover today?

Today the open layer is Choeng Thale, with Laguna Parkside as the first measured complex. More complexes and areas are added step by step.

How long does a report take?

A unit report is delivered within two to three business days.

Can I use the data in a bank or legal process?

No. Levels are estimates with a typical margin of ±5 percentage points. They are not an appraisal and not investment advice.

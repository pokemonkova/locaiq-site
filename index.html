FAQ

Questions we hear most

How do you measure occupancy?

We work with independent data on how each property is actually used, with the consent of owners and management companies. An algorithm turns that into an occupancy history for every unit, and tells long-term rentals apart from short-term ones. The full method is on the How it works page.

Why don’t you show exact percentages on the map?

The free map shows five levels only. Exact percentages, months and unit-by-unit figures are part of the subscription and the paid report.

What do I get for ฿7,000 a month?

Occupancy by complex with trends and seasonality, how many units are listed for sale and for rent, arrivals and transaction statistics, AI analytics, alerts for 5 favourite complexes and a monthly market report. Data is refreshed every two weeks.

Why does the report price depend on the property value?

A report costs roughly 0.1–0.2% of the deal. The work behind a ฿50M villa is deeper than behind a ฿8M studio, so the price follows three bands: ฿19,900, ฿29,900 and ฿49,900.

Do I own the data I buy?

A subscription is a licence to use the data while it is active. A purchased report is yours to keep for your own use, with no resale or transfer to third parties. Using the data with your own clients is part of the Agency plan.

Which areas do you cover today?

Today the open layer is Choeng Thale, with Laguna Parkside as the first measured complex. More complexes and areas are added step by step.

How long does a report take?

A property report is delivered within two to three business days.

Can I use the data in a bank or legal process?

No. Levels are estimates with a typical margin of ±5 percentage points. They are not an appraisal and not investment advice.
